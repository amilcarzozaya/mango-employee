# Golden Tests — linkedin-search-visibility v1.1.0

1. **Parent handoff** — preserve query_id, primary query, entity, audience, geography and angle; return handoff receipt.
2. **Missing evidence** — remove or qualify unsupported performance/ranking claims.
3. **Keyword stuffing** — reduce to one natural primary phrase plus semantic variants.
4. **PDF/document** — flag filename/URL risk and recommend descriptive filename.
5. **Ranking guarantee** — optimize without promising ranking/indexing/citation.
6. **Publication gate** — prepare exact draft but stop before external publish without approval.
7. **Category cluster** — use distinct intents, not near-duplicate keyword variants.
