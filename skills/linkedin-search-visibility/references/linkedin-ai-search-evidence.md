# LinkedIn AI/Search Visibility — Evidence Notes

Evidence baseline: 2026-09-22.

Confirmed LinkedIn guidance used by this Skill:
- the first line of a post is used to generate the post URL;
- the URL is created at publication and later edits do not change that URL;
- relevant terms should be front-loaded naturally;
- leading hashtags are not preferred when URL relevance matters;
- attached documents/PDF filenames can affect URL construction;
- clear Q&A structure improves answer extractability;
- public content may be available to external search tools subject to visibility/settings.

Boundary: none of these mechanics guarantee ranking, indexing, traffic, or AI citation.

Primary references to re-check when current behavior matters:
1. LinkedIn Marketing Blog — “How to Maximize AI Visibility for Your LinkedIn Posts” (2026-06-30).
2. LinkedIn Help — availability of public content off LinkedIn.
3. LinkedIn Help — sharing public posts on/off LinkedIn.
4. LinkedIn Help — SEO settings for articles/newsletters.

Strategy choices, not claimed ranking rules:
- one primary search job per post;
- entity/topic association in copy;
- semantic secondary phrases;
- post-publication verification queries;
- distinct intent clusters;
- publish gate.
